# Ashtar Trade Test Centre

Ashtar Trade Test Centre is a technical training institute in Multan, Pakistan. We offer practical, hands-on training and trade-test preparation for skilled workers preparing for Gulf jobs.

## Our Courses

- [6G Welding (SMAW/TIG/MIG)](https://www.ashtartradetest.com/courses/welding-training)
- Electrician
- Pipe Fitter
- Pipe Fabricator
- Mechanical Fitter
- HSE
- Civil Surveyor

Our training focuses on trade-test preparation, so students are ready for international assessments and interviews. We also provide Job Placement Assistance to help graduates find Gulf jobs.
